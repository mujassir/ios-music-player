### Description

Cassette plays the music files you already own, fully offline. No streaming, no subscriptions, no account and no Wi-Fi needed.

PICK YOUR FOLDERS
• Choose one or more music folders from the Files app or iCloud Drive
• Folders inside folders are found automatically
• Your files stay where they are and are never copied or uploaded
• Plays MP3, M4A, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, WAV and AIFF

FIND ANY SONG
• Browse your library folder by folder, with artwork and song lengths
• Sort by name, date modified or date created
• Search songs, artists and albums at once

LISTEN ANYWHERE
• Keeps playing in the background and with the screen locked
• Lock Screen and Control Center controls, including scrubbing
• Shuffle, repeat one and repeat all
• Plays straight through a folder and starts over from the top
• Picks up where you left off

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your music never leaves your device. Cassette doesn't collect any data, show ads or need an account.

### App Review notes

Cassette plays audio files that are already on the device. It has no account or sign-in. Its only network request is a once-a-day update check to Apple's App Store lookup service (app ID and country code only).

To test: open the Files app and put a few audio files (MP3 or M4A) in a folder under On My iPhone or iCloud Drive. Open Cassette, tap Choose Music Folder, and pick that folder. Tap a song to play it; the mini player at the bottom controls playback, and tapping the title opens Now Playing. Audio continues in the background and can be controlled from the Lock Screen.
