# Cassette 1.1.0 (build 3)

## What's New in This Version

Paste into App Store Connect → iOS App 1.1.0 → What's New in This Version.

---

CarPlay: browse your folders and play songs on your car's screen, with Now Playing controls.

Siri: say "Play Road Trip in Cassette" or "Shuffle Cassette" to start music by voice, even when the app is closed. Cassette's actions also appear in Shortcuts and Spotlight.

Reset: clear your whole library from the main screen and start fresh. Your music files are never deleted.

---

## App Review notes (additions for 1.1)

Add below the existing notes in App Review Information → Notes.

---

NEW IN 1.1
a) CarPlay (CarPlay Audio entitlement granted for this app): connect to CarPlay or the CarPlay Simulator. Cassette shows Library (folders) → songs → Now Playing. Tapping a song starts playback and opens Now Playing.
b) Siri: after adding the sample "Music" folder, say "Hey Siri, play Road Trip in Cassette" (a folder) or "Hey Siri, play Windows Down in Cassette" (a song), or "Hey Siri, shuffle Cassette". Siri finds the music in the user's own library on the device; no data leaves the device.
c) Reset: on the main screen, tap Reset under Add Folder and Rescan, then Reset Library. All folders are removed from Cassette (files are not deleted) and the welcome screen appears.
